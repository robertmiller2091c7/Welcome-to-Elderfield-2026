## 🕹️ About

Welcome to Elderfield is a cozy horror farming RPG set in a secluded town where strange residents, eldritch beings, and unsettling mysteries shape everyday life. Build and manage a farm, explore the town and its dangerous locations, befriend or romance locals, gather resources, and survive turn-based battles against Lovecraftian creatures. The Windows release is planned for September 10, 2026, developed by Ill-Advised Records and published by Kwalee.

A single-player experience for players who enjoy farming sims, relationship-driven RPGs, atmospheric mysteries, and cosmic horror.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🌾 **Horror Farming** — Grow unusual crops, manage a farm, raise livestock, and process goods.
- 🗺️ **Mysterious Exploration** — Explore Elderfield, its surrounding wilderness, and forbidden locations.
- ⚔️ **Turn-Based Combat** — Use equipment, resources, and strategic buffs against terrifying creatures.
- 💬 **Relationships** — Befriend, romance, and complete tasks for Elderfield’s unusual townsfolk.
- 🎣 **Daily Activities** — Fish, cook, mine, forage, craft gear, and investigate strange events.
- 🎨 **Hand-Drawn Horror** — Experience horror manga-inspired artwork with a dark lo-fi soundtrack.
